# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want a friendly, beginner-safe place to learn Hermes Agent.

## Our standards

- Be kind and clear
- Welcome questions from new users
- Focus on verified facts and working links
- No harassment, hate, or personal attacks
- No dumping secrets, tokens, or private data

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content that breaks these rules, and may block repeat abuse.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, and discussions in this repository.

## Contact

Open an issue on this repo for conduct concerns related to this list. This is not the official Nous Research support channel.
